# Caio Marchesani

I am a hedge fund manager, trader, engineer and all-round investor. I hold an MEng in Mechanical Engineering from University College London and have spent more than fifteen years in energy and macro markets. After working for Deutsche Bank and hedge funds, I became a partner at Oil Analytics and then set up my own quantitative macro energy fund in London. Outside finance I invest in and build businesses. I founded Acai Berry Foods, the açaí café brand that has grown from London to the UAE and New York.

I write about markets, macro data and energy on [caiomarchesani.com](https://caiomarchesani.com/). The Python behind the data pieces is in [market-and-macro-python](https://github.com/marchesanicaio/market-and-macro-python).
